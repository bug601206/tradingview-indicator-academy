# Contributor Rights Decision

**Status: founder decision recorded on 2026-10-14 — copyright-retaining CLA plus DCO.** The acceptance workflow remains inactive pending qualified legal review and an attributable acceptance mechanism. External code pull requests will not be merged until activation. This document is not legal advice.

## Comparison

| Model | Participation | Trust | Proprietary relicensing | Administration | Clarity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DCO 1.1 | Easy: signed-off commit | High; contributor keeps rights | No automatic right to proprietary relicense | Low | Certifies origin/authority, not broad relicensing rights |
| Copyright-retaining CLA | Extra acceptance step | Depends on narrow, transparent terms | Can provide defined additional rights | Medium/high | Strong if reviewed and consistently recorded |
| Copyright assignment | Highest friction | Often lowest community trust | Strongest centralized flexibility | High | Ownership transfers; not recommended silently |
| No additional agreement | Easiest | High | Requires later permission from every relevant holder | Low now, high later | Inbound contribution is GPL-only by project policy |

## Recommendation

The founder has selected a **narrow, copyright-retaining CLA plus DCO** because preserving future dual-licensing or proprietary-licensing options is an explicit goal. Do not use copyright assignment. DCO sign-off is an authorship/provenance attestation in addition to—not as a substitute for—the CLA.

The repository may be published for use and discussion before the acceptance workflow is active, but external code contributions must not be merged. Documentation-only participation may be evaluated separately when legally appropriate.

## Commercial and GPL effects

- GPL permits commercial use and commercial support.
- The founder retains copyright in the founder's contributions; contributors retain theirs.
- A DCO does not assign copyright and does not itself grant proprietary relicensing.
- A CLA may grant additional rights only as clearly stated and accepted.
- Existing GPL grants to legitimate recipients cannot simply be revoked.

## Founder-approved CLA policy text — acceptance not active

The founder approved this policy direction and text on 2026-10-14. It is not open for contributor acceptance until qualified legal review is complete and the official repository activates a versioned, attributable acceptance workflow.

1. **Definitions.** “Project” means TradingView Indicator Academy. “Contribution” means the original code, documentation, tests, or other material intentionally submitted by the contributor for inclusion in the Project; it excludes unrelated inventions, private research, account data, and material not intentionally submitted.
2. **Ownership.** The contributor retains copyright ownership of the Contribution.
3. **GPL license.** The contributor licenses the Contribution for distribution as part of the Project under GPL-3.0-only and confirms that downstream recipients receive the rights of that license.
4. **Additional license to the official project holder.** The contributor grants the official project holder a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, distribute, and license the Contribution, including under other open-source or proprietary terms.
5. **Transparency.** The contributor understands that the additional license may enable commercial or proprietary relicensing. It does not remove GPL rights already granted for published GPL versions.
6. **Authority.** The contributor represents that they created the Contribution or have sufficient authority to grant these rights, and will identify relevant third-party material and license terms.
7. **Patents.** Any patent license must be narrowly reviewed before adoption; this draft does not silently claim unrelated patents or inventions.
8. **No obligation.** Submission does not require acceptance, payment, employment, support, or use of the Contribution.
9. **No unrelated rights.** No rights are granted in unrelated work, private research, trademarks, personal data, or confidential information.
10. **Record of acceptance.** Acceptance must be explicit, attributable to the contributor, versioned, and retained by the official project owner.
