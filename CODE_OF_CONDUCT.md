# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free project for people of every background, identity, experience level, profession, and market viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence and implementation choices respectfully.
- Welcome beginners and explain specialized terminology.
- Give attribution and disclose uncertainty or conflicts of interest.
- Protect private information and security reports.
- Accept review and correction without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, impersonation, spam, market manipulation, deceptive performance claims, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the official repository owner through the contact method published in the repository profile or GitHub moderation tools. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants proportionately.

The founder is the final enforcement authority for the official project. Enforcement should be documented privately, applied consistently, and disclose only what is necessary.

## Scope

This policy applies in official project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly. It does not control lawful independent forks, though those forks must not imply official endorsement.
