# Future Commercial Options

GPL-3.0-only allows commercial use. The project can remain community-first while supporting lawful commercial activity.

## Feasible options

- Paid installation, maintenance, migration, and technical support.
- Professional education and training based on the open project.
- Custom indicator research or educational-content services, with client confidentiality handled outside the public repository.
- Hosted services that interact with the project, subject to analysis of whether covered code is conveyed or modified.
- Enterprise integrations and accessibility/localization services.
- Separately developed proprietary modules that are genuinely independent works with clean interfaces and no incompatible incorporation of GPL-covered derivative code.
- Dual licensing of code for which the founder/project holder possesses sufficient copyright and patent rights.

## Rights required

The founder can separately license the founder's own contributions. Proprietary relicensing of a combined version containing external contributions requires appropriate additional permissions from every relevant rightsholder, a valid CLA granting those rights, or replacement of those contributions. A DCO alone is insufficient.

Publicly distributed GPL versions remain available under the grants already made; a later business model cannot retroactively revoke compliant recipients' rights.

## Boundaries

- Do not add paywalls, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, payments, or tracking as part of this preparation.
- Do not promise commercial success or verified trading performance.
- Keep confidential client work, credentials, private data, and unreleased research in separate private repositories.
- Do not assume a separate folder/repository converts a GPL-covered derivative into proprietary code.
- Obtain qualified legal advice before dual licensing, combining with proprietary components, trademark enforcement, or material enterprise distribution.
